# Official Epic Manny Simple resource

Publisher: Epic Games, Inc. The included FBX was exported from the unmodified
`SKM_Manny_Simple` skeletal mesh in the Unreal Engine 5.8.2 installation's
`Templates/TemplateResources/High/Characters/Content/Mannequins/Meshes/` folder.
Its shared skeleton is `SK_Mannequin`. Both source assets were hash-matched with
the isolated export project's copies. The FBX was exported with Epic's native
SkeletalMeshExporterFBX, with no animation or retargeted user motion added. Two document-URL metadata
strings were replaced with repository-relative URLs; every other FBX byte is
unchanged. Original-export and distributed-file hashes are both recorded.

See [provenance and hashes](PROVENANCE.json) and [Epic license notice](LICENSE.txt).
The applicable [Unreal Engine EULA](https://www.unrealengine.com/eula/unreal)
defines Templates content as Examples and permits its redistribution under
section 5(b). Epic's terms and notices remain attached to this resource.

The reference import has 88 bones and 48,705 mesh vertices. Missing author-side
normal/environment images are not bundled; the importer reports them and keeps
material colours. No additional texture, Unreal project, engine binary, Mixamo
character, Unity character or user animation is included in this directory.
